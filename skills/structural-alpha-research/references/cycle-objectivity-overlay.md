# Cycle / Objectivity Overlay

Use this reference when a task asks to combine chokepoint research with a global-cycle / objectivity overlay, or when the base thesis needs a stronger cycle, liquidity, low-ROE elasticity, and objective-evidence pass.

This file is a methodology overlay, not a views corpus. Do **not** attribute a conclusion to an external source unless the user supplies a primary-source quote or a loaded source contains it. Treat external projects only as benchmark inspiration: primary-source anchoring, explicit source-vs-inference separation, and objective evidence discipline.

## 1. What This Adds To The Base Model

Base chokepoint research asks: **demand → constrained supply → low attention → value capture → catalyst → anti-thesis**.

The overlay adds five checks before a name can be treated as high-quality:

1. **Cycle / technology inflation:** What global technology, capex, policy, or physical-supply cycle is creating pricing power, lead-time extension, utilization, or profit-pool migration?
2. **Comparative advantage:** Why is this company / region / process advantaged in the constrained layer rather than merely “related”?
3. **Low-base elasticity:** Is profitability depressed for a recoverable reason such as supply-demand pressure, early R&D, underutilized capacity, or product-mix transition? Avoid confusing structurally poor economics with optionality.
4. **Liquidity and execution reality:** Can the market structure support research monitoring without turning the thesis into a gap/limit-board/liquidity trap?
5. **Objectivity:** Which claim is primary evidence, which is cross-check, and which is only a method-based inference?

## 2. Research Sequence

Run this sequence after the initial narrative but before Shortlist:

1. **Global cycle map** — identify the changing technology route, capex cycle, policy cycle, physical shortage, or customer spend cycle.
2. **Inflation signal** — look for price increase, lead-time extension, tight utilization, backlog, prepayment, gross-margin improvement, or competitors’ capacity commentary.
3. **Chain compression** — reduce the chain to the layer where value capture is shortest and least substitutable.
4. **Comparative-advantage test** — explain why the candidate is better positioned than adjacent theme names.
5. **Low-base elasticity test** — ask whether incremental revenue or gross profit can repair ROE / margins / operating leverage from a depressed base.
6. **Liquidity / market-structure test** — check liquidity, crowding, limit-board risk, and whether current price action has already consumed the misclassification.
7. **Objective exit** — state what would prove the cycle, bottleneck, company exposure, or elasticity wrong.

## 3. Low-ROE / Low-Base Elasticity

Low ROE is attractive only when it is **recoverable**:

- **Supply-demand suppressed:** industry pricing or utilization was depressed, but the company retains capability and customer access.
- **Pre-investment suppressed:** R&D, qualification, capacity, or platform spend depresses current profit while the cycle is moving toward the company’s product.
- **Mix-transition suppressed:** a new higher-value product can change margin mix and operating leverage.

Downgrade when low ROE reflects permanent issues: weak product, poor governance, destructive dilution, commodity competition, customer loss, obsolete technology, or unreliable accounting.

Minimum output:

```text
低基数弹性：可修复 / 待验证 / 不成立
原因：供需压制 / 预先研发 / 产品结构 / 经营杠杆 / 其他
关键证据：收入、毛利率、费用率、研发、产能利用率、订单或客户验证
```

## 4. Backlog / Unit-Economics Quality

Do not treat backlog or order growth as proof of value capture by itself. Before using backlog as a Shortlist input, check:

- **Margin path:** latest gross margin / contribution margin and credible bridge to positive or improving economics.
- **Cash conversion:** whether growth converts into operating cash flow or requires structurally rising working capital.
- **Funding dependence:** debt, equity issuance, government loans, customer prepayments, or other external financing needed to deliver the backlog.
- **Cancellation / repricing risk:** whether orders are firm, cancellable, fixed-price, cost-pass-through, or dependent on policy milestones.
- **Capacity / qualification risk:** whether production, yield, certification, or customer qualification can support the implied ramp.

If these are unresolved, label the backlog claim as **待核实事实** and cap the candidate at Watchlist / Excluded depending on severity.

## 5. Objectivity Labels

Use these labels in compact form when the thesis is uncertain:

- **原始证据** — filings, announcements, official presentations, transcript quotes, customer/supplier documents.
- **交叉验证** — customer, supplier, competitor, industry data, product pricing, lead times, market data.
- **按框架推演** — conclusion inferred from the method because direct evidence is incomplete.
- **待核实事实** — specific company, financial, price, capacity, order, customer, or ROE claim not yet verified.

If a user asks for a style or method simulation, answer as framework inference, not attribution:

> 下面是按该类全球周期/客观证据/低基数弹性框架做的推演，不代表任何外部来源的原始观点。

## 6. Output Additions

Add these fields to shortlist or memo outputs when relevant:

- **周期驱动：** technology route / capex / policy / physical shortage / pricing cycle.
- **客观通胀证据：** price, lead time, backlog, utilization, margin, or customer spend data.
- **比较优势：** why this company captures value.
- **低基数弹性：** recoverable ROE/profitability repair or “不成立”.
- **订单/积压质量：** margin path, cash conversion, cancellation/repricing, and financing dependency.
- **流动性与拥挤：** current market context and execution risk.
- **客观反证：** the data that would force downgrade or exit from the research bucket.

## 7. Hard Caps

- No objective cycle evidence → cannot be a strong Alpha candidate.
- No comparative advantage → theme narrative or Watchlist.
- Low ROE without recoverability proof → red flag, not upside.
- Backlog without unit-economics and cash-conversion proof → Watchlist / Excluded.
- Liquidity/crowding dominates evidence → Watchlist / 回避交易 for trading scenario.
- Method simulation without direct evidence → label as 推演 and cap below strong confidence.
