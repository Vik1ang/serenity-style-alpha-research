# Domain Extensions

Load the relevant section only for the user’s asset class or industry.

## AI Infrastructure

Focus areas:

- GPU, ASIC, HBM, advanced packaging;
- CPO, silicon photonics, optical modules, DSP, EML, CW laser, InP substrate, optical test equipment, fiber/cable;
- switches, datacenter power, UPS, transformers, grid equipment, cooling/liquid cooling;
- neoclouds, AI/HPC datacenters.

Key questions:

- Which layer is actually capacity constrained?
- How long is expansion/qualification?
- Who are qualified suppliers?
- Are hyperscalers or OEMs locking capacity/prepaying?
- Is it already visible in revenue/backlog/margins?

## Semiconductor Supply Chain

Focus areas:

- materials, equipment, testing, foundry, advanced packaging, substrates, photoresist, specialty gas, power semis, memory, chiplet, CoWoS-like bottlenecks.

Key questions:

- Did technology route change demand for a specific node/material/tool?
- Is capacity scarce and hard to qualify?
- Are customer switching costs high?
- Does localization/import substitution have verified orders, not just policy narrative?

## Datacenter Power

Focus areas:

- power capacity, transformers, switchgear, UPS, generators, gas generation, grid interconnection, storage, liquid cooling, PUE, land/power resources.

Key questions:

- Is AI datacenter expansion constrained by power availability or equipment lead time?
- Which companies control scarce equipment/resources?
- Are there real orders/backlog and price/margin improvement?
- Can capacity and working capital support growth?

## Crypto Mining / AI-HPC Conversion

Focus areas:

- BTC miners, mining sites, power resources, hashrate, mining difficulty, ASIC prices, miner revenue, AI/HPC conversion, datacenter retrofit capex, financing/dilution.

Key questions:

- Can the site truly support AI/HPC density, uptime, cooling, networking, and customer requirements?
- Are there signed customer contracts or only LOIs/narrative?
- Is power scarce and contractually controlled?
- What capex and dilution are needed?
- Does AI/HPC revenue offset mining cyclicality?

## A/H/HK/US Supply-Chain Equities

Focus areas:

- A-share optical modules, PCB, liquid cooling, grid equipment, semiconductor materials/equipment, compute leasing;
- HK small-cap technology, upstream materials, equipment suppliers, localization chains.

Key questions:

- Business purity and revenue mix;
- customer qualification and actual order quality;
- valuation already price in or not;
- concept speculation vs financial-statement impact;
- liquidity and governance risks.

## Commodities / Physical Supply-Demand

Focus areas:

- mine supply, refining/smelting, shipping/logistics, inventories, term structure, marginal cost, substitution, policy constraints.

Key questions:

- Is the bottleneck geology, permitting, processing capacity, transport, inventories, or financing?
- Is demand recurring or one-off restocking?
- Does term structure validate scarcity?
- Which equities/assets have operational leverage without excessive balance-sheet risk?
