# Elasticity and Factor Checklist

Do not output A/B/C/D ratings. Use this file only to quantify elasticity or structure the factor checklist.

## Elasticity Formulas

- **Demand Elasticity** = Potential Incremental Revenue / Current Revenue
- **Profit Elasticity** = Potential Incremental Profit / Current Profit
- **Valuation Elasticity** = Potential Incremental Profit / Current Market Cap

If current profit is near zero or negative, do not mechanically create infinite elasticity. Explain whether incremental gross profit can cover fixed opex and create an operating-profit inflection.

## Serenity-Style Factor Checklist

| Factor | Core Question | Evidence Needed |
|---|---|---|
| Certain Demand | Is downstream demand real and growing? | capex, orders, backlog, shipments, ASP, utilization, customer commentary |
| Constrained Supply | Is the link hard to expand/replace? | supplier count, capacity cycle, yield, qualification, equipment/material limits |
| Low Attention | Is the company still misclassified? | low coverage, old label, small cap, misunderstood segment, stale financials |
| Value Capture | Can bottleneck status hit financials? | revenue mix, pricing power, margins, operating leverage, cash flow |
| Catalyst | What forces recognition soon? | earnings, customer qualification, production ramp, guidance, contracts, industry data |
| Anti-Thesis | What can kill the setup? | substitution, dilution, weak demand, customer loss, competition, valuation crowding |

## Evidence Strength

- **Strong** — primary evidence + cross-check + current financial/market data.
- **Medium** — primary evidence exists but one major link needs future validation.
- **Weak** — mostly inferential or social-media driven.
- **Insufficient** — cannot map demand, bottleneck, exposure, or financial transmission.

## Hard Caps

- Social-media thesis only → not a strong alpha candidate.
- No verified demand → usually theme narrative.
- No revenue mix/exposure data → potential only, not strong.
- Third-order exposure → theme narrative unless proven otherwise.
- Severe dilution/debt/liquidity risk unresolved → cannot be strong.
- No current market cap/valuation → valuation elasticity is data insufficient.
